Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to teach your child, we never sell or share it for advertising, only the tutor and your child can see your child’s work, and camera monitoring during tests never records or saves any photo, video or sound. You can ask us to see or delete your child’s information at any time.

Who we are

Kulkarni Academy is an online Maths and Science tutoring service run by Aditya Kulkarni, based in India. For any privacy question or request, email kulkarniacademyyy@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 93734 38129.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: when you ask about classes, the details you send us (name, year/grade, school board, city or time zone, what help you need) arrive in our WhatsApp or email. The website itself does not store them.
  • Student account: your child’s name, login email, password (stored securely by our login provider; we can’t read it), year/grade, school board and time zone.
  • Learning records: class times and links, homework and the work your child uploads, test answers (including typed answers and photos of working), marks, the tutor’s comments, and practice results.
  • Test integrity notes: how many times a test was left (switching apps or leaving full screen), and, for tests with monitoring switched on, the short notes described below.

We do not use advertising, tracking cookies or analytics that follow you across websites. The student portal keeps your child signed in using the browser’s own storage.

Camera & microphone monitoring during some tests

For some tests the tutor may switch on monitoring, so students work on their own. When it is on:

  • The student is told before the test starts and must confirm that a parent or guardian knows.
  • The camera and microphone are checked only on the student’s own device, while the test is open.
  • No photo, video or sound is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Only short text notes are sent to the tutor, for example “face not visible for 20 seconds at question 4”, “more than one person in view” or “talking heard”.
  • These detailed notes are deleted automatically after 10 days; only a count (for example “2 notes”) stays with the test result.
  • Notes are hints for the tutor to talk to the student about, not automatic penalties.

Why we use it

  • To run classes, set and mark homework and tests, and show your child their progress.
  • To keep tests fair.
  • To share progress with you (for example a progress report you ask for).

Who can see it

  • Your child sees only their own classes, work and results.
  • The tutor sees the work and results of their students.
  • Nobody else. We never sell your information or share it for marketing. Other students can never see your child’s information.

Where it is stored

The website is hosted by Vercel, and student accounts, records and uploaded files are stored with Supabase, a secure cloud database provider. These providers may store data on servers outside your country. Connections are encrypted, and access is restricted so that each student can only ever reach their own records.

How long we keep it

  • Learning records are kept while your child studies with us, so progress can be tracked.
  • When homework, a test or a student account is deleted, the related files are removed automatically within about two days.
  • Camera & microphone notes are deleted after 10 days.
  • If your child stops classes, ask us and we will delete their account and records.

Children’s privacy and your rights

Our students are children, so we only create a student account after speaking with a parent or guardian. Parents can ask us at any time to:

  • see the information we hold about their child,
  • correct anything that is wrong,
  • delete their child’s information, or
  • stop using camera & microphone monitoring for their child.

Email kulkarniacademyyy@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days. If you are in the UK, you can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If we change how we use information, we will update this page and tell parents of current students.
